Organizing a large-scale developer conference like DevFest Florida teaches you engineering lessons you simply can't find in textbooks.

When you bring together hundreds of developers, sponsor partners, keynote speakers, and community volunteers, the challenges mirror large-scale distributed systems:

1. **Failure modes happen; plan for graceful degradation:** Audio-visual glitches, last-minute speaker cancellations, or Wi-Fi bottlenecks test your incident response. Having contingency tracks and clear playbooks ensures the attendee experience remains seamless.
2. **Mentorship multiplies impact:** Giving first-time technical speakers a platform to present on AI, web standards, and cloud architecture builds a supportive ecosystem where everyone grows.
3. **The power of developer community:** Software engineering can often feel isolating behind an IDE. In-person developer gatherings remind us of the shared curiosity and collaborative spirit that drives our industry forward.
