In this session presented at Orlando Code Camp 2025, we explore signals as the modern primitive for fine-grained reactivity in front-end frameworks.

### Topics Covered
* Fundamental anatomy of signals and dependency graphs.
* Historical evolution from Knockout to SolidJS, Vue, and Angular.
* Implementing a pure JavaScript reactive signal runtime in under 50 lines.
* Practical migration strategies from zone.js-based Angular apps to signal-based components.
